## 1. Voice of Jarvis: Sarvam for ever
**Wish:** the Sarvam voice must be the voice of Jarvis for life. Sarvam free credit will end. The Edge voice sounds strange.

**Truth:** a NEW sentence in exactly the Sarvam voice needs the Sarvam API. There is no free way to copy that voice. What we can do:

| Part | What | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 197 | Save the audio of every sentence Sarvam speaks on the phone; play it again for free. Make about 150 common lines once | Greetings, confirmations and errors repeat all day. They cost credit only one time |
| 198 | Long replies speak only 2 sentences, the rest stays text. Count the characters sent to Sarvam, warn at Hussain's limit. Switch `Sarvam voice or silence` | Fewer characters = credit lasts longer. When Sarvam fails Jarvis shows text instead of the Edge voice |
| 193 | Backup order: Sarvam, saved audio, Edge only if allowed, phone voice | Jarvis never goes mute by accident |

**Later:** when Hussain buys a paid Sarvam plan nothing in the code changes: same key, same voice.
**Not planned:** copying the Sarvam voice with an own model. Not checked against Sarvam terms, heavy and weak on a phone. **Read the Sarvam terms about saving audio before 197.**

## 2. People by voice (Part 199)
**Wish:** the phone knows who speaks, saves the name, next time says the name and the last talk. Also accent (UP, Bihar) and boy / girl.

- **Yes:** voice print (Part 144) + name. Unknown voice: Jarvis asks `aap kaun?`. Saved only after the Yes tap. Next time: `Haan Faisal, last time ...`. Works while a conversation is already listening (Jarvis never opens the mic alone, Part 144).
- **No:** accent and boy / girl as saved facts. They are a guess from sound and often wrong; a wrong label about a person is worse than none. Instead Hussain can say `ye Faisal hai, UP ka` and it is saved as a note in his words.
- Owner voice not recognised: Part 192 (3 samples, adjustable limit).
